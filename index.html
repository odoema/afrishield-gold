Who We Are

A Licensed Bridge Between African Gold and Global Markets

We operate only inside formal channels. Every lot we handle carries a verifiable paper trail from source to export.

Our Model

AfriShield Gold Ltd offers international buyers a transparent, fully documented alternative to informal gold trade in East and Central Africa. We trade only with licence holders recognised under Uganda's Mining and Minerals Act, 2022, and, in the DRC, with registered cooperatives, négociants and comptoirs.

Our headquarters and trading desk are in Kampala. Gold is bought from licensed operators in Uganda and validated sites in eastern DRC, refined locally to 99.9% purity, then exported under a permit for each consignment. We do not buy gold of unknown or high-risk origin.

  • Mineral dealer's licence (precious metals) and registration on the Mining Cadastre
  • Named source mines or cooperatives in validated zones
  • Independent assay (fire assay / XRF) for every consignment
  • CEEC certificate and DRC export permit for every Congolese lot
  • Uganda import and export permits, with royalty payment records
  • KYC / AML screening of every counterparty

Mines

Where the Gold Comes From

We buy from three kinds of licensed operation, from giant open-pit mines that move rock with 400-tonne trucks to small cooperatives working by hand. Every one is verified before we buy a gram.

Large-scale open pit

Industrial mines with shovels, haul trucks and processing plants. We buy doré or refined gold through formal offtake agreements with licensed operators.

Small-scale licensed mines

Locally owned operations with a valid mining licence, smaller excavators and simple processing. Site visits and production records on file.

Artisanal cooperatives

Registered cooperatives in validated, conflict-free zones. We pay fair, documented prices and support safer, mercury-free practices.

How We Work

Transparent Chain of Custody

From cooperative to certified export, every step is documented and verifiable.

  1. Source Verification

    Licensed mines and registered cooperatives in validated zones. Site visits and risk assessments on file.

  2. Licensed Purchase

    Buy only from licence holders. DRC lots enter Uganda with a DRC export permit, CEEC certificate and a Ugandan import permit.

  3. Assay & Refining

    Independent assay, royalty payment, then refining to 99.9% at a licensed Ugandan refinery.

  4. Permitted Export

    Export permit and certificate of analysis per consignment. Insured, tracked transport with the full due diligence file.

Never trust a licence on paper alone

Forged gold licences and export papers are a common tool in gold scams across the region. Before any gold deal:

  • Check the seller's licence on Uganda's Mining Cadastre and Registry System or directly with the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Development.
  • Confirm the company exists on the URSB registry and that names match on every document.
  • Insist on an independent assay at a lab you choose, and never pay "release", "tax" or "clearance" fees to strangers.
